2. Notes, widget and story exports

Fewy stores your notes on your device. The Fewy widget can access recent notes through the app’s shared storage and display them on your Home Screen. These features work without a Fewy account. Your note text and story content stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

When you export a story, Fewy creates an image file on your device. If you save or share it through the iOS share sheet, you choose the destination or recipient. The chosen service’s privacy terms apply to any further processing by that service.

We process this information to provide the app features you use under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Notes remain on your device until you delete them in Fewy or remove the app and its data. You manage exported files wherever you saved or shared them.

3. Privacy choices and advertising

Managing your choices with Google UMP

Fewy uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether it needs to show a privacy message and to let you make or change your choices. UMP stores your choice on your device as a consent signal. Fewy reads that signal to decide whether to request ads. UMP checks the status of your choice with Google as part of managing your preferences, including when you decline advertising consent. Google identifies Google LLC as the operator of its consent management platform.

You can change your choice through “Ad privacy options” in “Styles” when the option is available. Withdrawing consent takes effect for future processing.

Advertising with Google AdMob

Fewy uses Google AdMob to fund the app through native ads in “Styles.” Our AdMob contracting entity is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google is the only advertising partner currently listed in Fewy’s European consent message.

If you choose “Do not consent,” Fewy sends no ad request to AdMob. You can continue using the note and export features. If you decline ad personalization but grant the permissions required for non-personalized ads, Fewy can request those ads. If you withdraw consent and the requirements for advertising are no longer met, Fewy removes the displayed ad and stops making further ad requests. Programmatic limited ads are disabled.

Depending on the features used and your choices, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses, device identifiers, diagnostic and performance data, information about ads, and interactions with ads. Google documents uses such as ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention. Where we rely on your consent to process personal data for advertising, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Where device storage or access for advertising requires consent, we ask for it through the privacy message.

Google sets retention periods according to the type of data and why it was collected. See Google’s data retention policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google may process advertising data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Google states that it relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers of EEA personal data to the United States through its advertising services. For certain transfers where an alternative transfer mechanism does not apply, Google’s advertising terms provide for EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google’s advertising data transfer information and Google’s controller-to-controller terms, Appendix 1.

4. Optional purchases through Apple

If your version of Fewy offers an optional Studio purchase, Apple handles the purchase and payment through the App Store. Fewy checks your purchase status through StoreKit to unlock or restore features under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Fewy does not receive payment details such as your card number. See Apple’s privacy policy.

5. Contact

If you contact us, we use your contact details and message to respond. We rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contract-related inquiries and on our legitimate interest in responding to other inquiries under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. We keep messages for as long as needed to handle your inquiry or to meet applicable legal retention requirements.

6. Your rights

Subject to the applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future and object under Article 21 GDPR to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the authority where you live or the Danish Data Protection Agency.

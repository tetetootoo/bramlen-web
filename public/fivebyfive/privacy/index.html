Puzzle progress

Five by Five saves your current puzzle, progress, elapsed time, moves, resets, and tutorial status on your device. It uses these records to provide the play and resume features you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where applicable). Five by Five does not send these records to a Bramlen server. Removing the app and its data removes the progress stored in that installation.

Camera and scorecards

With your permission, Five by Five can take a photo to include in your scorecard. It processes the photo on your device to provide the scorecard feature you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where applicable). You can remove the photo from the preview before sharing. If you save the image to Photos or use the iOS share sheet, the copy is handled by Photos or your chosen destination, including any syncing you enable.

Advertising

Five by Five uses Google AdMob to display non-personalized banner ads. The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to determine which privacy choices apply and to present and store those choices. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, where consent is required, the app requests ads only after consent for device storage and Google is recorded. If you decline those permissions, the app does not request a banner. Where the “Ad Privacy Options” item appears, you can revisit your choices in the question-mark menu.

Google may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, crash logs and performance information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, maintain its services and prevent abuse. Where consent is required, we rely on your consent for this advertising processing (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can find more information about Google’s processing, retention and international transfers in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Support and privacy requests

Cloudflare Email Routing forwards messages sent to support@bramlen.com to our support mailbox. If you email us, we use your address, message, and any attachments to respond. We rely on our legitimate interest in handling inquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, where applicable). We retain correspondence as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry or meet applicable legal obligations.

Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. To submit a request, email support@bramlen.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority or the Danish Data Protection Agency.
